# Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

_Last updated / 最后更新：2026-08_

## English

TrustExt is designed to collect **nothing**.

- **No personal data is collected.** TrustExt does not gather, transmit, or sell any personal or usage information.
- **No analytics or tracking.** There are no trackers, telemetry, or third-party analytics in this extension.
- **No remote servers.** The extension runs entirely on your device. The curated list ships inside the extension package.
- **Local storage only.** The single `storage` permission is used solely to remember your language preference (English or 中文) on your own device. This value never leaves your browser.
- **Outbound links.** When you click an extension's **Source** link, your browser opens that public repository (e.g. GitHub) in a new tab, exactly as if you had typed the address yourself. TrustExt does not add tracking to these links.

Because TrustExt is open source (MIT), you can verify every one of these claims by reading the code in the public repository.

Questions or concerns: please open an issue at
https://github.com/wushu75/TrustExt/issues

## 简体中文

TrustExt 的设计原则是**不收集任何数据**。

- **不收集个人数据。** 本扩展不会收集、传输或出售任何个人信息或使用数据。
- **无统计与追踪。** 扩展中不含任何追踪器、遥测或第三方统计代码。
- **无远程服务器。** 扩展完全在你的设备本地运行，精选列表随扩展一同打包。
- **仅本地存储。** 唯一申请的 `storage` 权限仅用于在你本机记住语言偏好（English 或 中文），该数据永远不会离开你的浏览器。
- **外部链接。** 当你点击某个扩展的「源码」链接时，浏览器会像你自己输入网址一样，在新标签页打开对应的公开仓库（如 GitHub）。TrustExt 不会向这些链接添加任何追踪参数。

由于 TrustExt 完全开源（MIT 许可），你可以通过阅读公开仓库中的源代码来核验以上每一条声明。

如有疑问，请在此提交 issue：
https://github.com/wushu75/TrustExt/issues
